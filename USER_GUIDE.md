# 🥽 Welcome to the VR Chentai Training!

Welcome to your virtual training experience! If you've never used Virtual Reality (VR) before, don't worry. This guide is designed to help you step-by-step so you can learn how to operate and interact with the Chentai machinery safely and easily.

---

## 1. Putting on the Headset
*   **Adjust for Comfort:** Place the headset over your eyes and pull the straps over your head. Adjust the dial or straps until the screen is clear and comfortable. 
*   **Look Around:** In VR, you are inside the world! Feel free to physically turn your head to look up, down, left, and right just like you would in real life.
*   **Stay Planted:** It's best to stand in one place with your feet planted, or sit in a chair.

## 2. Using Your Virtual Hands (The Controllers)
You will hold one controller in each hand. Use them as your virtual hands:
*   **Pointer Finger (Trigger Button):** Squeeze the trigger button on the front of the controller to **Point**, **Select** items on menus, or **Push** buttons on machines.
*   **Middle Finger (Grip Button):** Squeeze the button on the side of the controller to **Grab** and hold objects (like tools or machine parts). Let go of the button to drop them!
*   **Thumb (Joystick):** Push the joystick forward to **Move** or "teleport" to a new spot around the room without having to physically walk. Move it left or right to quickly snap your body's direction.

## 3. How the Training Works
Once you are inside the virtual room, you will go through step-by-step tasks:
*   **Follow the Guide:** You may see a virtual instructor character who will guide you or give you a "thumbs up" when you do something correctly.
*   **Read the Labels:** Look closely at the machine. Important parts will have glowing text labels telling you what they are.
*   **Hands-on Practice:** You will be asked to physically reach out and push buttons, or grab materials (like foils) and place them exactly where they need to go in the machine. 

## 4. The Assessment (Test Mode)
After you finish the tutorial and practice levels, you will enter the **Assessment Mode**. Here, you will try to complete the steps on the machine by yourself to test what you've learned. Remember:
*   Take your time, there's no need to rush.
*   Make sure pieces "snap" safely into their correct spots.

## 5. VR Safety & Comfort (Important!)
*   **Take a Break:** If you ever feel dizzy, warm, or slightly sick, simply close your eyes, take off the headset, and take a 5-minute break. This is completely normal for beginners, and your brain just needs a moment to adjust!
*   **Clear your Space:** Make sure you aren't near any real-world desks or walls so you don't bump into them when reaching for a virtual button.

---
*Enjoy your training! You are completely safe in the virtual world, so have fun exploring and learning.*
